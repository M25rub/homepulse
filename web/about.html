About HomePulse

Why it exists, how it works, and what is real today.

The problem

Smart devices sometimes fail quietly: a lock that did not turn, a light that ignored the message. You often find out later, by accident, and nothing tells you which devices keep failing.

The fix

HomePulse sends the command, checks the real state of the device, tries again if needed, uses a backup route, and tells you what really happened. Then it remembers, so you can see which devices are reliable and which need fixing.
